About AETOVA

Local problems.
World-class solutions.

We build affordable, intelligent software for the African institutions that serve millions of people.

Understand local problems deeply. Then build world-class solutions, locally.

AETOVA was founded to bridge the technology gap in underserved communities. We saw African churches, institutions serving millions, being left behind by global tech trends and offered expensive Western tools that were never designed for them. So we built EKKLESIA AI from the ground up.
